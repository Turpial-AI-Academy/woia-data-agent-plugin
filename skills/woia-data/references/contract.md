# Generic Data contract


Five bounded responsibilities: recover applicable accepted agreements; maintain coherent identities/source relationships; establish suitability for intended use; coordinate changes/imports/migrations; resolve integrity problems and sustain verifiable controls. Apply proportionally, not as mandatory approval stages. Ordinary authorized capability operations proceed directly. Data is not a universal writer, routine-read proxy, business-state master or source of Finance/Legal acceptance.

Core v0.5.7 owns Tasks, Due Work, autonomous cross-department delivery and organization-resource resolution. Receivers own their Task and authority; preserve scope, correlation, evidence and competent acceptance. No duplicate Task engine. Data Governance supplies deterministic reviews/evaluation/source-map capability. Identity supplies shared identity; Domain Contracts supplies semantic relations and evaluation fixtures. Resolve admitted compatible resources and private versioned organization bindings. Missing required resources block only affected consequential operations.

Keep structured facts, original Evidence, Inference, Core work state and external authoritative sources distinct. A Source Authority Map assigns source/writer, scope, version, freshness/conflict/cutover responsibilities; it does not grant authority. Clean import/extraction/quality review does not establish a business fact. Finance retains monetary acceptance and Ledger/Payments; Legal retains competent applicability/formalization; business owners retain semantics/KPIs. Customer Service/Communications alone execute external-person contact and scheduling. Data dispatches no contact/payment.

Normalization knowledge covers 1NF, 2NF, 3NF, BCNF, 4NF and 5NF/PJNF with all candidate keys and actual functional, multivalued and join dependencies. Lossless reconstruction and enforceability are separate obligations; finite samples do not prove them. The [accepted normalization contract](normalization-contract.md) fixes the immutable target for each scoped relation. The authenticated host resolves its current source identity, revision and digest; requests cannot select a policy or target. Review obligations are cumulative through the accepted target. Derived views preserve source, freshness and permission contracts.

Evaluate security, integrity, quality, completeness, lineage and freshness against accepted purpose/policy. UNKNOWN/STALE/CONFLICT require owned reconciliation, never debt, good standing or accepted truth. Source changes and cutover require responsible owners/versioned agreements. Coordinate imports, opening-position acceptance, reconciliation and verified recovery with Software, Technology and business owners. Recovery never reverses external effects or revives revoked access. Retain UNKNOWN outcome evidence and reconcile before retry; same correlation with changed intent is a conflict.

No DBMS/backend, hosting, law, retention period, SLA, account/fee policy or vendor is selected. These pure guards and schema/prose are not runtime enforcement, storage qualification or actual resource loading. Physical enforcement, clean installation/continuation/upgrades, provider/host qualification and cross-domain E2E remain later independent gates. No publication/admission, Operator E2E, fresh G6/G7 or Production Ready claim.
